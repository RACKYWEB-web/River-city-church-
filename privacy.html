1. Who We Are

Rackyweb Nexus ("we," "us," "our," or "the Platform") is a global digital commerce and education platform founded by Edward Prince and operated worldwide. We provide e-commerce marketplace services, AI-powered learning tools, digital academy programmes, startup ecosystem support, and creator community features.

This Privacy Policy governs all data we collect through our website, products, and services. By using Rackyweb Nexus, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

🔒 Our Commitment: We will never sell your personal data to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. Your information is used solely to deliver, improve, and personalise our services for you.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly:

  • Name, email address, and password when you create an account
  • Billing and payment information for purchases and subscriptions
  • Profile details including bio, skills, and professional background
  • Content you upload to the marketplace (products, descriptions, media)
  • Messages and communications you send through our platform or contact forms
  • Course progress, quiz responses, and learning activity

Information Collected Automatically:

  • IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers
  • Pages visited, features used, and session duration
  • Referring URLs and search queries used to find our platform
  • Technical performance logs and error reports

Information from Third Parties:

  • Social media profile data when you connect external accounts
  • Payment verification data from our payment processor partners
  • Analytics data from trusted third-party analytics providers
3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Creating and managing your account and profile
  • Processing purchases, subscriptions, and marketplace transactions
  • Delivering courses, AI tools, and platform features
  • Personalising your learning experience and product recommendations
  • Sending transactional emails, receipts, and account notifications
  • Sending our newsletter and updates (only with your consent)
  • Analysing platform usage to improve features and performance
  • Detecting and preventing fraud, abuse, and security threats
  • Complying with our legal obligations under applicable law
  • Issuing verified certificates and tracking learning achievements

📧 Marketing: We only send marketing communications with your explicit consent. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email, or by contacting us directly.

4. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted third parties who help us operate the platform (e.g., payment processors, cloud hosting, email delivery), bound by confidentiality agreements
  • Legal Requirements: When required by applicable law, court order, or governmental authority
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of business assets, with prior notice to you
  • Community Features: Profile information you choose to make public on our platform (leaderboards, marketplace listings, etc.)
  • Your Consent: Any other sharing will only occur with your explicit written consent
5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience. The types of cookies we use:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for core platform functionality. Cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use the platform (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and personalisation choices.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to serve relevant content. You can opt out at any time.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect platform functionality.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmitted to and from our platform
  • AES-256 encryption for stored sensitive data including passwords
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Strict role-based access controls for employee data access
  • Comprehensive incident response procedures for data breaches

In the event of a data breach affecting your rights, we will notify you within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, in accordance with applicable law.

  • Right to Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Right to Object: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw any consent you have given at any time
